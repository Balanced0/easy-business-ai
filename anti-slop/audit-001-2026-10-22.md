## Numbered Findings List

### Hard Gate (Priority: HIGH)

1. **R-37 — Design Direction Required**
   - **Target:** Project root
   - **Reason:** No `DESIGN.md` or written brand guidelines exist; the design defaults to AI-standard dark midnight-indigo with ungrounded purple glows.

2. **R-02 — Copywriting: Em Dashes in Meta & Page Titles**
   - **Target:** `src/routes/index.tsx`, `pricing.tsx`, `_app.dashboard.tsx`, `_app.about.tsx`, `_app.billing.tsx`, `_app.customers.tsx`, `_app.intelligence.tsx`, `_app.scan.tsx`, `_app.upload.tsx`, `_app.integrations.tsx`, `_app.inventory.tsx`, `_app.competitors.tsx`, `_app.privacy.tsx`, `login.tsx`, `signup.tsx`, `onboarding.tsx`
   - **Reason:** Forbidden em dash (`—`) character is used in page title tags and HTML metadata across 16 route files.

3. **R-02 — Copywriting: Em Dashes in In-Page Copy & Text**
   - **Target:** `src/routes/index.tsx` (L243, L339), `src/routes/_app.about.tsx` (L34, L62, L67, L95, L127), `src/routes/_app.intelligence.tsx` (L33, L63, L183), `src/routes/_app.scan.tsx` (L143, L211), `src/routes/_app.upload.tsx` (L121), `src/routes/pricing.tsx` (L358, L374), `src/routes/_app.dashboard.tsx` (L364, L406)
   - **Reason:** Forbidden em dash (`—`) used as sentence connectors, parentheticals, and dividers in English and bilingual user-facing copy.

4. **R-17 & R-38 — Data & Numbers: Fluctuating Invented Metric Cards in Landing Hero**
   - **Target:** `src/routes/index.tsx` (L130–160, L268–325)
   - **Reason:** Hero bento mockup calculates random pseudo-numbers on a 2.2-second interval ($240,000+ sales, shifting revenue, random deltas), creating fabricated statistics disguised as live product metrics.

5. **R-17 & R-36 — Data & Claims: Unsourced Market Opportunity Statistics**
   - **Target:** `src/routes/_app.about.tsx` (L150–168)
   - **Reason:** Presents specific macroeconomic figures ("$47B market", "4.2M sellers", "18% CAGR") without citations, data sources, or evidence.

6. **R-23 & R-38 — Visual Assets & Placeholders: Fictional Name in Form Input**
   - **Target:** `src/routes/pricing.tsx` (L400)
   - **Reason:** Uses fictional person name `Jane Doe` in the checkout cardholder input placeholder instead of an honest label (`Your Name` or empty).

7. **R-26 — Interactive Elements: Simulated Integrations Testing & Connection**
   - **Target:** `src/routes/_app.integrations.tsx` (L138, L149)
   - **Reason:** Integration test and save buttons execute fake `setTimeout` delays and toast notifications without saving credentials or contacting an API.

8. **R-26 — Interactive Elements: Simulated Inventory Restock & Promo Actions**
   - **Target:** `src/routes/_app.inventory.tsx` (L54–61)
   - **Reason:** Restock and promo buttons execute a dummy `setTimeout(600)` delay with no persistent backend state change or order dispatch.

9. **R-26 — Interactive Elements: Non-Functional Credit Purchasing Flow**
   - **Target:** `src/routes/_app.billing.tsx` (L42–52)
   - **Reason:** Pricing pack buttons display an informational toast explaining Stripe is offline rather than offering a functional action or honest coming-soon disablement.

10. **R-27 — UI States: Contextless "No Data" Empty States**
    - **Target:** `src/routes/_app.customers.tsx` (L81–83) and `src/routes/_app.inventory.tsx` (L123–125)
    - **Reason:** Data views render bare "No data" / "No items" labels with no explanation of cause or immediate action to resolve the empty state.

11. **R-32 — Keyboard Accessibility: Removed Focus Outline Without Replacement**
    - **Target:** `src/components/ui/command.tsx` (L47)
    - **Reason:** Command input uses `outline-none` without an explicit focus-visible ring or outline, leaving keyboard navigators without a focus marker.
