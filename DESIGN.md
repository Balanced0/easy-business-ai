# Design Direction: EasyBusiness AI

## Product Identity

- **Product:** EasyBusiness AI (ইজিবিজনেস)
- **Target Audience:** Small and medium ecommerce sellers (Daraz, Shopify, independent stores) across South Asia, primarily Bangladesh.
- **Personality:** Practical, trustworthy, bilingual (Bangla & English), focused on commerce clarity rather than AI hype.

## Visual Direction & Mood

- **Direction:** Market Paper.
- **Mood:** Warm, decisive, and human. It should feel like a reliable commerce workspace, not a generic operations dashboard.
- **Palette:**
  - Light ground: Warm paper (`#FAF6F0`) with midnight ink (`#19202A`).
  - Dark ground: Deep ink (`#171D28`) with pale paper text (`#F8F2EC`).
  - Primary: Clay (`#C94E26` in light mode, `#FF9365` in dark mode) for the main affirmative action and selected states.
  - Semantic: Deep green for positive business states, amber for inventory attention, and rose for destructive or stockout states. Green success is never the only status signal.
- **Typography:**
  - Headings / Display: `Noto Serif Bengali`, with `Noto Sans Bengali` as the immediate fallback. Use normal letter spacing and generous line height so Bangla vowel signs and conjuncts are not clipped.
  - Body / Interface: `Noto Sans Bengali`, with `Hind Siliguri` and system fonts as fallbacks. This is the default for mixed Bangla and English UI text.
  - Technical values: `Noto Sans Mono` may be used for Latin-only IDs. Bangla values fall back to `Noto Sans Bengali`.

## Dials

- **ENERGY: 2** (Balanced: clear focal points, calm ground plane, no ungrounded glowing orbs)
- **RHYTHM: 2** (Structured: distinct section treatments with varied weight, no identical card repetitions)
- **MOTION: 1** (Minimal: purposeful hover and focus transitions only, zero continuous loops or pulsing pings)
